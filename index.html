Welcome

Most math is just problem solving. Good problem solving is something that isn't taught that much. Some people are really good at working with numbers, some people have a hard time with those, too. Either way, problem solving can help make math less frustrating for everyone!

How it works

You will look at different shapes and say how they are related to each other. This may seem easy, or it may seem hard. The words you will use to talk about these shapes are the same kinds of words that show up in word problems. Talking about these shapes will also help you talk about numbers! This website will take you through 5 Units. You will be able to solve word problems and write equations all without doing any number math. By the end, doing math with numbers will be a breeze.

Your Goal

Each skill has a goal. When you reach it, you move on. Your progress is shown on a chart so you can see yourself getting better. Your goal shows you exactly what you are working towards. Once you complete Units 1–3, you will start solving word problems. First you'll solve without numbers, then with numbers. Along the way you may need help with some other skills — we'll let you know. Your first step is to go through the tutorial! Happy problem solving!
